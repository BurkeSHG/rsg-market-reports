# Markets

One folder per market. Every market here has passed the quality gate; see QUALITY-GATE.md.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `handoff.csv` | The build sheet. About 130 rows in the page's own section order, so you can read down it as you work. `Status` is FILLED (final), CONSTANT (identical in every market, copy from Waco) or PENDING (not supplied yet, the Note says who supplies it). |
| `companies.csv` | The contractor scorecard table, sorted by review count. Columns match the table on the Waco page. |
| `assets.csv` | What we need back from you: local photographs with a shot description, alt text and caption, plus one dated screenshot of the Google results page. |

`ALL-CITIES.csv` is every field across every market side by side, for comparison.

Everything in these files is publicly observable: business names, Google review counts and
ratings, map positions, whether a company runs ads (Google publishes an Ads Transparency
Centre), whether AI engines name them, and what is on their own website.

The eight photograph rows per market are the only PENDING items that are genuinely yours.
