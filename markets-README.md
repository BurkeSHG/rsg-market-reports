# Market data

The per-market build sheets are not in this repository. They name individual HVAC
contractors with their review counts, map positions, advertising and AI visibility,
which is prospect research and stays internal.

Ask Burke for the market you are building and you will get three files:

- `handoff.csv` - the build sheet, about 125 rows in the same section order as the
  page, each marked FILLED, CONSTANT or PENDING
- `companies.csv` - the contractor scorecard table
- `assets.csv` - the photographs and screenshot we need back from you

Everything needed to understand the structure before you have those files is in this
repository: the saved reference page, the layout teardown, and the field contract.
